About this Tool

Background

This academic reference tool is designed for sociology students and researchers. It provides quick access to prominent sociologists, their core theories, key research cases and major works, helping users build a systematic map of sociological thought.

Data Overview

The database covers both classical and contemporary sociologists, including theoretical schools, active periods, nationality, representative works and core research propositions. The dataset is continuously expanded and updated.
